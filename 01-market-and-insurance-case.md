# 1. Market and the Insurance Case — North America

Why an insurer will pay for your hardware, what the incumbents already own, and where the gaps are.
Researched 2026-10-14. **[V]** = vendor- or filing-published, **[E]** = estimate.

---

## 1.0 The two numbers that anchor every carrier conversation

**Water.** 1.50 claims per 100 house-years at **$15,400** average severity (2019–2023 average),
making water damage and freezing **22.6% of all homeowners losses** in 2023 — second only to
wind/hail and ahead of fire and lightning at 21.6%
([iii.org](https://www.iii.org/fact-statistic/facts-statistics-homeowners-and-renters-insurance),
on ISO/Verisk data). About **one in 60 insured homes** files a water damage or freezing claim each
year; [IBHS independently confirms](https://ibhs.org/interior-water/state-of-the-risk-interior-water-damage/)
one in sixty for 2016–2020. Severity is getting worse fast: non-weather water damage severity rose
**+63% from 2019 to 2025** while frequency *fell* 24% year-on-year (LexisNexis *U.S. Home Trends
Report*, July 2026, [via InsuranceNewsNet](https://insurancenewsnet.com/oarticle/costlier-claims-push-home-insurance-costs-up-for-all-even-those-who-never-filed-insurify)).
Amica, launching a program in October 2026, states the current figure as **one in 60 policyholders
per year with average claims exceeding $21,000**
([Insurance Journal](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/east/2026/10/09/888585.htm)).

**Electrical.** US residential building fires in 2023: **344,600 fires, 2,890 deaths, 10,400
injuries and $11.266 billion** in losses, of which **23,700 fires** were caused by electrical
malfunction — one of the four leading causes, up 2% over the decade
([USFA](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/statistics/residential-fires/),
[causes](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/statistics/residential-fires/causes.html)). Over 2014–2023 fires
fell 6% but **dollar loss rose 25%**.

> **Unverified:** NFPA's specific "home structure fires involving electrical distribution and
> lighting equipment" counts could not be retrieved — nfpa.org renders client-side and ESFI returned
> HTTP 403. Use the USFA figures above as your citable primary source and buy the NFPA report
> separately if you need its breakdown.

### Why this is getting worse structurally, not cyclically

IBHS attributes the trend to housing stock, not weather: median new-home size grew from ~1,500 sq ft
in 1980 to ~2,500 sq ft in 2015, homes with three full bathrooms are up nearly 10% since 2005, and
**modern washers and dishwashers last only 12–13 years**
([IBHS exec summary](https://ibhs1.wpenginepowered.com/wp-content/uploads/Interior-Water-White-Paper-Exec-Summary.pdf)).
More water fixtures per home, shorter appliance life. The peril compounds on its own.

### The demand-side gap — the single best slide you will ever show a carrier

The Hanover / Harris Poll surveyed 1,516 US homeowners in April 2023
([report](https://www.hanover.com/2023-home-water-damage-prevention-report)):

| Finding | Figure |
|---|---|
| Homeowners own a smoke detector | **84%** |
| Own a fire alarm | 51% |
| Own a security camera | 48% |
| Own a burglar alarm | 34% |
| **Own a water sensor** | **17%** (22% even at $100k+ income) |
| Unfamiliar with water sensors | **68%** |
| Have never heard of automatic water shutoff | **61%** |
| $100k+ households who would install **if given a discount** | **53%** |

And the risk is inverted relative to the spending: homeowners are **7× more likely** to suffer
plumbing-leak damage than fire, and water damage is **6× more common than burglary**. This is an
education problem, not a price problem — which is exactly why the insurer is the natural
demand-creation partner even for a retail SKU.

## 1.3 The incumbents

### Ting (Ting Labs, formerly Whisker Labs) — your most important comparable

**What it is.** A sensor that plugs into a standard outlet and **measures voltage 30 million times
per second**, analysing the waveform for micro-arcing — the precursor to most electrical fires — and
localising the source. Also monitors grid faults and does low-temperature/frozen-pipe warning.
Explicitly contains *"no microphones, cameras, motion detectors, speakers or other sensors"*, but
does carry an internal circuit-board temperature sensor. Two-minute DIY install.
([tingfire.com](https://www.tingfire.com/))

**Scale.** **1.4 million US homes** (Sept 2026), **40+ carriers across the US and Canada**, 30,000+
fires claimed prevented, "4 out of 5 home electrical fires prevented". Rebranded from Whisker Labs
on 21 July 2026 ([PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/whisker-labs-becomes-ting-labs-reflecting-the-name-more-than-one-million-homes-already-trust-302830396.html)).

**Price.** DTC **$99 including the first year, then $49/year**; the service is mandatory and includes
a **$1,000 repair credit** for Ting-identified hazards. Free for life if you get it through your
insurer.

**Connectivity and its limits.** 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only. Per
[PCWorld's review](https://www.pcworld.com/article/2772388/ting-sensor-with-fire-prevention-service-review.html):
incompatible with smart plugs, surge protectors or UPS; **no local audible or visual alarm**; and
**multiple panels require separate devices and separate subscriptions**. Electricians on
[Mike Holt's Forum](https://forums.mikeholt.com/threads/ting-arc-fault-device.2574896/) question
efficacy on multi-wire circuits and note it cannot trip a breaker. **Each of these is a wedge.**

**Funding.** Financing **led by State Farm Ventures** (Nov 2019), backed again Nov 2021.

**Strategic moves to note.** (1) **Verisure took an exclusive European licence** in Sept 2026 under
a co-development model — Ting supplies technology, firmware and cloud; Verisure manufactures, sells
and installs; launch early 2027 ([PR](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/verisure-widens-its-offering-with-exclusive-ting-labs-partnership-bringing-advanced-electrical-fire-prevention-technology-to-europe-302890518.html)).
(2) **Adaptive + Whisker Labs launched real-time parametric power-outage insurance** — the sensor
fleet became a reinsurance trigger. That is the data-product endgame, productised.

**And the one design decision to copy.** Ting **alerts but never interrupts the circuit.** That is
what keeps it out of UL 1699 (AFCI) — a standard that would add $60k–200k. See
[07-compliance-and-certification.md](07-compliance-and-certification.md).

### LeakBot / Ondo InsurTech (LSE: ONDO) — the only public unit economics in this market

**What it is.** Clips onto the **main water pipe**, no tools, five-minute self-install. Patented
**Thermi-Q** measures air *and* water temperature to infer flow: *"even the tiniest hidden leak
constantly draws colder water into your home from the underground pipe. This creates a small,
consistent temperature difference that normal water use doesn't."* Detects leaks as small as
**5 ml/minute**, anywhere on the main, including behind walls
([leakbot.io/how-it-works](https://www.leakbot.io/how-it-works)).

Physically this is a **clamp-on differential-temperature sensor** — two temperature sensors and a
spring clip. Extraordinarily cheap to build, no plumbing licence, no wetted materials, no shutoff
liability. **This is the method behind the recommended wedge**, which is also why §1.6 on patents
matters.

**The commercial model, stated most clearly by Westfield**
([westfieldinsurance.com/leakbot](https://www.westfieldinsurance.com/leakbot)): free device plus
**unlimited free repair visits for two years**, covering labour, parts and taxes, with **any
individual part over $250 excluded**. Eligible in Ohio, Indiana and Pennsylvania. And the key
sentence: *"the LeakBot program does not impact your homeowners coverage or premium. Westfield does
not currently offer a discount."* That is the purest expression of device-as-loss-control.

**The financials, and read these carefully** (FY ended 31 Mar 2026):

Revenue mix is **£3.8m recurring vs £0.8m one-off device fee** — about 82% recurring. NPS +88.
Capital reality: repeated dilutive raises, including a June 2026 refinancing and placing of ~£2.9m

**The lesson is not that the model is wrong** — recurring revenue grew 50% and US revenue 115%. It
is that **in-home repair fulfilment and sub-scale density destroy margin.** Launch monitoring-only,
partner for repair, and concentrate geographically: their own data shows margin moving 50% → 61%
purely from density.

## 1.5 Competitive white space

1. **No-Wi-Fi and poor-Wi-Fi homes.** Ting needs 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, fails behind a surge protector, and
   needs **one sensor per panel**. The only vendors who solved this — Alert Labs (cellular) and Eddy
   (LoRaWAN) — are **commercial-only**. A consumer-grade cellular or LoRaWAN device for insurer
   programs is unoccupied.
2. **Renters** — ~35% of US households. Nationwide gives homeowners a free device but only lets
   renters *buy at a discount*. LeakBot needs an accessible main; Flume needs a compatible meter.
   Unserved by every program found.
3. **Multifamily in-suite at scale.** The field splits into MEP-room commercial (WINT, Eddy, Alert
   Labs), per-unit Wi-Fi (Resideo), PropTech bundles (SmartRent) and one NYC specialist. Nobody owns
   *cheap, dense, in-suite, no-Wi-Fi, insurer-endorsed* — while >70% of operators call water their
   top claim cause.
4. **Condo/HOA sub-metering + leak + insurance in one product.** Boards are now writing leak
   liability onto unit owners ([Daily Herald](https://www.dailyherald.com/20260520/real-estate/is-condo-boards-new-rule-about-water-leaks-appropriate/));
   no incumbent combines sub-metering, detection and an insurance credit.
5. **Small commercial.** Chubb's average commercial water loss is **$89,000** and WINT targets the
   Empire State Building. Restaurants, clinics, small retail and small offices have no productised
   offer.
6. **Electrical beyond Ting.** It claims to be *"the only fire prevention product of its kind"* and
   has now locked Europe exclusively to Verisure. Unoccupied adjacencies: multifamily and commercial
   panels, no-Wi-Fi homes, renters, **multi-panel properties** (Ting charges per panel), and a
   **local audible alarm**.
7. **Canada** is the most open geography in this report. One carrier (PURE, Ontario) offers Ting;
   no LeakBot, Flume or Phyn Canadian insurer program exists; commercial is held by Eddy and
   Alert Labs.
8. **Insurer risk-data products.** Only one example of monetising the fleet as data exists (Adaptive
   + Whisker Labs parametric outage cover), while LexisNexis launched Location Intelligence for Home
   in May 2026 — carriers are actively buying predictive property models. Sensor-derived risk scores
   and first-notice-of-loss triage are a real second revenue line nobody in hardware has taken.
9. **There is no off-the-shelf North American NEMA 5-15 metered LoRaWAN or cellular smart plug.**
   Milesight's WS51x is EU/FR plug and CE-only despite supporting US915. A concrete, narrow,
   verifiable product gap.
10. **Older housing stock** is where both perils concentrate, yet no vendor serves homes with **no
    accessible main shutoff** or an incompatible/absent water meter.

## 1.6 Patent exposure — budget for this, do not discover it

The recommended wedge sits directly on an active patent family. Treat a freedom-to-operate opinion
as a P0 line item.

| Patent | Title | Assignee | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AU2016384312B2** | Fluid flow detection apparatus | **Leakbot Limited** | 2015-01-07 |
| **US10508966B2** | Water flow analysis | **Homeserve Plc** | 2015-02-05 |
| **US11299871B2** | Leak detection method and apparatus (**pressure-decay**, not thermal) | Homeserve Plc | 2017-10-05 |
| **US10352814B2** | Water leak detection using pressure sensing | **Phyn LLC** | 2015-11-10 |
| **JP7502260B2 / KR102801557B1** | Detection of discharges preceding fires in electrical wiring | **Whisker Labs** | 2018-03-20 |

Phyn's claim is worth understanding because it is a **signal-processing** claim, not a sensor claim:
leak identification from **pressure alone, from a single sensor**, via persistent turbulence in
specific frequency bands, monotonic pressure slope interrupted by regulator resets, pressure drop
during no-use periods, and abnormal variation in stable pressure. "High-rate pressure sensing" means
sampling an ordinary transducer fast enough to see turbulence spectra. The Whisker Labs family is
likewise a method claim on **voltage-cycle waveform** monitoring, active in US, EP, CN and JP.

> **Unverified:** US coverage of the AU2016384312 thermal family could not be resolved. Get a real
> FTO opinion before committing to the clamp-on thermal method — this is the one piece of diligence
> that could invalidate the recommended wedge.
